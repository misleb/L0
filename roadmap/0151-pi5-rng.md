## Why

L0S's `probes/driver/REQUEST-pi5-rng.md`. Its TLS 1.3 client keys from
`random`, which a Pi 5, with no virtio, had nothing to serve: its
`rng200` driver got "no device there" from `(region_open 70818758656
40)`. L0S's roadmap 0047, and 0048's last step, wait on it.

  0x28; a paragraph on its registers, and that the host sets none up

  `pi5-aarch64-devices.ll` written again from it; no interrupt
- `tests/virt/pi5/rng`: opens it, and ranges beside it that are none
- no emulator: checked by building and linking; a first run is L0S's
